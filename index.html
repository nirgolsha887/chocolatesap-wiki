Joining

Java Edition

  1. Open Minecraft and go to Multiplayer, then Add Server.
  2. Enter the address chocolatesap.minekeep.gg and click Done.
  3. Join. The server runs the latest version, and older versions can connect too.

Bedrock Edition

  1. Phone, console and Windows players can join the same worlds as Java.
  2. Go to Play, then Servers, then Add Server.
  3. Server address: chocolatesap.bedrock.minekeep.gg, port: 19132.
  4. Bedrock names show up with a dot in front, like .Steve.

Voice chat

The server has proximity voice chat. On Java, install the Simple Voice Chat mod to talk and hear the players near you.

First steps

  1. You start in the lobby. You come back here when you join and when you respawn.
  2. Press Escape on Java and click ChocolateSAP Menu, or type /menu (on Bedrock too). It has a shortcut to everything.
  3. Type /rtp to go somewhere random in the survival world and start playing.
  4. Set a home with /home so you can get back to your base. Everyone gets 5 homes.
  5. Check the sidebar on the right: it shows your money, chocolates and XP level.

Chocolates

Chocolates are the server's trophy currency. Your total is on the sidebar.

  • Win a duel: you get a chocolate.
  • Play: every 20 minutes of active play gives you 1 chocolate. Standing still (AFK) doesn't count.
  • Never lost: chocolates are a balance, not items, so dying or losing a fight never takes them.

You might also find chocolate bars: real items you can eat, like food. They don't add to your chocolates.

Quick Buy

Quick Buy is the player market. Open it with /ah.

Selling

  • Hold the item and type /ah sell <price>. Short prices work: 500, 1.5k, 2m, 1b.
  • You can have 5 items on sale at once, more with a rank (up to 60).
  • Items stay on sale until someone buys them. You get paid even if you're offline.
  • Click Your Items in Quick Buy to see your listings and take one back.

Buying

  • Click an item, then confirm on the green glass. Red cancels.
  • Use the hopper to sort: newest, cheapest, most expensive or by name.
  • Use the sign to search by name. Right-click it to clear the search.

Duels

  1. Invite someone with /duel <player>. They answer with /duel accept.
  2. Walk into the duel portal. You fight in your own private copy of the arena.
  3. Pick what you bring with /kit. Your survival items stay safe in the lobby while you fight.
  4. Win and you get a chocolate.

Settings

Type /settings (or use the Settings button in the menu). Each switch only changes what you see.

  • Sidebar: show or hide your money, chocolates and XP lines.
  • Above heads: show other players' money, chocolates or level above their heads. Turn on any of the three.

On Java the settings open as a window with checkboxes and a Save button. On Bedrock they open as a menu.

When you die

  • You keep your XP levels.
  • Your chocolates are never lost.
  • Your items drop where you died, so go back for them.
  • You respawn in the lobby.
